## Executive Overview {#overview}

The explosion of mobile internet access and digital payments across India has driven a surge in instant digital lending. Alongside regulated fintech platforms partnered with licensed banks and NBFCs, a massive parallel market of **unregulated, predatory lending apps** has emerged.

These rogue operations—often distributed via direct APK downloads, social media ads, or fraudulent app stores—exploit vulnerable borrowers with promises of "instant ₹5,000–₹10,000 loans without credit checks."

Upon installation, these apps gain access to smartphone contacts, photo galleries, and location data. Borrowers receive only a fraction of the loan after exorbitant upfront deductions, followed 6 days later by demands for repayment at 300%+ APR.

When repayment is delayed, recovery syndicates weaponize stolen phone contacts and photo galleries, sending morphed, obscene images to family members and colleagues.

The **Reserve Bank of India (RBI)** has established strict **Guidelines on Digital Lending** to dismantle these operations and protect consumers.

## Statutory Framework: RBI Digital Lending Guidelines {#statutory-framework}

Under the **RBI Guidelines on Digital Lending (September 2022)**:

### 1. The Regulated Entity (RE) Anchor Rule
Digital Lending Apps (DLAs) and Lending Service Providers (LSPs) cannot operate independently. Every digital loan must be underwritten and funded directly by a licensed **Regulated Entity** (a bank or RBI-registered NBFC).
- All loan disbursements and repayments must flow directly between the borrower’s bank account and the Regulated Entity's bank account, without passing through any third-party pool account.

### 2. Strict Ban on Smartphone Media & Contact Access
Under Clause 4 of the guidelines, DLAs are **strictly prohibited from accessing smartphone contact lists, photo galleries, media storage, call logs, and device file systems**:
- Apps may only access location data (one-time for KYC verification) and camera (for live selfie KYC verification).
- Any app requiring contact list or storage permissions to approve a loan is operating in direct violation of RBI directives.

### 3. Mandatory Key Fact Statement (KFS)
Lenders must deliver a standardized, transparent **Key Fact Statement (KFS)** before the loan agreement is signed. The KFS must explicitly state:
- The all-inclusive **Annual Percentage Rate (APR)**.
- Detailed itemization of all fees, processing charges, and penal rates.
- The name of the partnering bank/NBFC and the designated Grievance Redressal Officer.

### 4. Mandatory Cooling-Off / Look-Up Period
Borrowers must be granted a statutory cooling-off window (typically 1 to 3 days for loans under 7 days, or 3 days for longer tenures) during which they can exit the loan by repaying the principal without penalty.

## How to Identify an Illegal Predatory Loan App

| Evaluation Metric | Legitimate RBI-Regulated DLA | Illegal Predatory App |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Partner NBFC / Bank** | Prominently named with RBI registration no. | Vague or fictitious foreign company names |
| **Smartphone Permissions** | One-time camera & location only | Demands access to Contacts, Photos, SMS, Storage |
| **Disbursement Account** | Directly from bank/NBFC account | Transferred via individual UPI handles / unverified wallets |
| **Tenure** | Standard terms (3, 6, 12, 24 months) | Ultra-short tenures (6 or 7 days) |
| **Key Fact Statement (KFS)**| Provided prior to agreement execution | Completely absent; fees deducted upfront |
| **Distribution** | Verified Google Play Store developer | Direct APK links via WhatsApp, Telegram, or SMS |

## Actionable Escalation & Legal Defense Roadmap {#worked-analysis}

If you or a family member are subjected to extortion or morphed photo harassment by illegal digital lending apps:

### 1. Discontinue Extortion Payments Immediately
Paying extortion money does not stop harassment; predatory syndicates demand further payments once they identify a paying victim.

### 2. Revoke App Permissions & Uninstall
Immediately navigate to device settings:
- `Settings` → `Apps` → Select Rogue App → `Permissions` → Revoke all permissions.
- Uninstall the app and run a reputable mobile security scan.

### 3. File a Formal Cyber Crime Complaint
Lodge a report on the official Government of India cyber crime portal:
- Website: **`https://cybercrime.gov.in`** or call the National Cyber Crime Helpline at **1930**.
- Upload screenshots of threatening WhatsApp chats, extortion calls, and payment transactions.
- Offences involve Sections 66E (privacy violation), 67 (obscene content) of the Information Technology Act, 2000, and extortion provisions of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS).

### 4. Report to the RBI Sachet Portal
Submit a regulatory report on the RBI’s anti-illegal entity portal:
- Website: **`https://sachet.rbi.org.in`**.
- Sachet coordinates enforcement actions between state police, cyber cells, and the RBI.

## Actionable Borrower Checklist {#actionable-checklist}

- [ ] **Check RBI NBFC Registry:** Cross-reference the lender’s corporate name against the official list of registered NBFCs on `rbi.org.in`.
- [ ] **Audit Android App Permissions:** Ensure no financial or calculator app on your phone has ongoing background access to Contacts or Photos.
- [ ] **Warn Family & Contacts:** Send a broadcast message to family and colleagues: *"A fraudulent loan app has illegally accessed my contact list and is sending fake extortion messages. Please ignore and block all calls."*
- [ ] **Never Hand Over Bank OTPs:** Legitimate digital lenders never request debit card ATM PINs or transaction OTPs over the phone.
- [ ] **Preserve Digital Evidence:** Save unedited screenshots of APK download URLs, UPI transaction IDs, and recovery phone numbers for police filings.

## Common Pitfalls & Non-Compliances {#common-pitfalls}

1. **Borrowing from a New App to Pay an Old Rogue App:** This creates a cascading debt trap with multiple extortion rings harassing the borrower simultaneously.
2. **Deleting WhatsApp Chats in Panic:** Threatening messages constitute primary forensic evidence for cyber crime investigations; export chats with media before blocking numbers.
3. **Believing Threats of Police Arrest for Illegal App Debts:** Unlicensed apps cannot invoke legal courts; their operations are entirely criminal extortion outside the law.
