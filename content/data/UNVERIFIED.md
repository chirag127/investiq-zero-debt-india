# Unverified Claims and Fact-Verification Backlog

This document records legal, regulatory, financial, and procedural claims that have been flagged as unverified, obsolete, or non-statutory during editorial review. Claims listed here **MUST NOT** be presented as current facts on ZeroDebt India.

---

### 1. "Collection agents can visit workplaces without prior notice"
- **Status**: INVALID / PROHIBITED.
- **Finding**: Under RBI Master Direction – Fair Practices Code for Lenders and Digital Lending Guidelines, recovery agents are prohibited from visiting workplaces without prior notification or causing public embarrassment. Borrowers' designated home address is the primary contact location unless specifically agreed otherwise. Calling or visiting workplaces to humiliate the borrower is an explicit violation.

### 2. "Settling a credit card loan removes all negative remarks from CIBIL after 1 year"
- **Status**: FALSE / DECEPTIVE CLAIM.
- **Finding**: Credit Information Companies (CICs) in India maintain historical records for 7 years under CICRA 2005. A "Settled" status signifies a waiver/haircut and remains flagged for 7 years unless the borrower pays the residual waived balance in full and gets the lender to report it as "Closed".

### 3. "Non-payment of personal loans leads to immediate arrest"
- **Status**: FALSE / MISLEADING SCARE TACTIC.
- **Finding**: Default on unsecured personal loans is a civil matter of contract breach, not a criminal offence. Criminal proceedings only arise under Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act (for dishonoured cheques) or Section 25 of the Payment and Settlement Systems Act (for dishonoured eNACH/ECS mandates) when a specific legal notice is issued and unpaid within 15 days.

### 4. "Banks can charge 2% foreclosure charges on floating-rate home loans"
- **Status**: OBSOLETE / ILLEGAL.
- **Finding**: RBI circulars and the *RBI (Pre-payment Charges on Loans) Directions* explicitly prohibit banks, HFCs, and NBFCs from levying foreclosure charges or prepayment penalties on all floating-rate term loans sanctioned to individual borrowers for non-business purposes.

### 5. "eNACH bounce cannot lead to any legal action"
- **Status**: FALSE.
- **Finding**: While Section 138 of the NI Act applies to paper cheques, Section 25 of the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007 (PSSA) establishes equivalent criminal liability (up to 2 years imprisonment or fine up to twice the dishonoured amount) for electronic funds transfers bounced due to insufficient funds.
